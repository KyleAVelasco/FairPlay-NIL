What the report gives you

Deal Score

One number from 0 to 100 and a verdict, so you can tell quickly how one-sided the deal is.

Key terms

Pay, length, exclusivity, who can end the deal, and what you owe the brand, pulled out of the legal wording.

Flagged clauses

Each one quoted and highlighted in the contract, rated by how serious it is, with why it matters and what to ask for.

Missing protections and questions

What a fair deal would include that this one leaves out, and questions to take to the brand or your compliance office.
