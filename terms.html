1What FairPlayNIL does

FairPlayNIL reads the text of a name, image and likeness (NIL) agreement and compares it against a fixed set of rules: wording that is commonly unfavorable to athletes, terms that protect athletes, and protections a fair deal usually includes. It then gives the contract a Deal Score from 0 to 100, lists the key terms, and explains each flagged clause in plain English.

The rules look for specific wording. A clause written in unusual language may not be caught, and a clause that matches a rule may be harmless in context.

5Privacy

What stays on your device. When you choose a file or paste text, the contract is read by code running in your own browser. It is not uploaded to FairPlayNIL or to any other server. Reviews you save, including the contract text, are stored in your browser's local storage on that device only. They do not sync between devices, and no one else can see them.

Deleting your data. Use Delete on any saved review, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Once deleted, it cannot be recovered, because no copy exists anywhere else.

What is not collected. FairPlayNIL has no accounts, sets no cookies, and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The site loads its fonts and code from its own address only, so no other company receives a request when you use it.

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page, for security and to keep the service running. FairPlayNIL does not receive those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Installing the app. If you add FairPlayNIL to your home screen, your browser stores a copy of the site's files so it can open offline. Your reviews remain in local storage on that device, as above.
